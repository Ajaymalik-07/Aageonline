# AageOnline — Consent

## Consent Principles

Consent must be specific, informed, recorded, and tied to the purpose for which it was collected where consent is the applicable legal basis.

## Business Registration

The registration experience should disclose:

- account creation
- required information
- applicable terms
- privacy information

## Business Claim

Claiming a business must establish that the claimant is authorized to manage the business.

The verification method and evidence requirements must be documented.

## Paid Position

Before payment, the user must see:

- business identity
- market
- target position
- amount
- paid visibility disclosure
- applicable payment/finality terms

The user must actively confirm the purchase.

## Communications

Marketing communications must use appropriate consent or other lawful basis as applicable.

Users should have a clear way to manage communication preferences.

## Records

Consent and relevant acknowledgements should be auditable with:

- user
- purpose
- version
- timestamp
- source/context
- withdrawal/update where applicable
