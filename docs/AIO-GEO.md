# AageOnline — AIO / GEO

## Objective

Make AageOnline's useful public information understandable and extractable by search engines and answer engines while maintaining factual integrity.

## Entity Structure

Public pages should clearly establish:

- AageOnline
- business
- category
- location
- market
- paid visibility position

## Answer-Oriented Content

Create useful pages answering questions such as:

- What are the top visibility positions for interior designers in Jaipur?
- How does AageOnline ranking work?
- How can a business move up a category ranking?
- What does a paid visibility position mean?

## Trust

Answer-oriented content must distinguish:

- paid visibility
- verification
- reviews
- business facts

These are separate signals.

## Structured Information

Use consistent headings, descriptive text, internal links, canonical URLs, and supported structured data.

## Principle

Optimize for useful answers, not keyword manipulation.
