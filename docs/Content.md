# AageOnline — Content

## Content Principles

Content must help users understand businesses, markets, and the AageOnline visibility model.

## Core Content Types

- Guides
- Questions
- Glossary
- Market explainers
- Category pages
- Location pages
- Business profiles
- Product/how-it-works content

## Tone

- clear
- confident
- transparent
- useful
- non-hype

## Avoid

- unsupported “best” claims
- fake statistics
- fake reviews
- misleading urgency
- keyword stuffing
- thin location pages

## Business Content

Business profiles should prioritize factual information:

- name
- category
- location
- description
- services
- website
- social links
- contact options where appropriate
- verification status where applicable
- paid visibility position where relevant
